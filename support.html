Color Puddle Support

Help for grown-ups

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea for a picture? Email colorappforkids@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Saving a picture to Photos

Tap the 📸 button while coloring. On iPad choose Save Image; on Android choose your photos or files app.

Where are my pictures?

Everything your child makes is saved automatically on this device. Open My pictures from the bottom of the home screen. Pictures are not stored online, so deleting the app also deletes them.

Basic or Advanced tools

Open Grown-ups (answer the grown-up question), then choose Basic tools (big and simple) or Advanced tools (layers, smudge, shapes and more). The 🧸/🎨 button on the left of the coloring screen switches too.

Privacy

Color Puddle has no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and works offline. Read the privacy policy.
