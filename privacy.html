Color Puddle Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Color Puddle collects no information at all. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet, except for the App Store or Google Play when a grown-up buys or restores the unlock. Everything your child makes stays on your device.

What the app stores

Pictures your child colors or draws, and app settings (such as sound on/off and Basic or Advanced tools), are saved only in the app's own storage on your device so your child can keep working on them. We cannot see them. They are removed when you delete them in My pictures or delete the app. If your device backs up apps (iCloud or Google backup), this storage may be included in your own backup, under your account and that provider's privacy terms.

What the app collects

Nothing. Color Puddle does not collect, send or share any personal information or usage data. It contains no third-party code for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social media. The only links that leave the app (this privacy policy and the support page) are in the Grown-ups area, behind a grown-up question.

Photos for pixel art

"Make it from a photo" turns a picture into pixel art. A grown-up or child picks a photo (or takes one with the camera on iPad). The photo is turned into colored squares right on the device; the photo itself is never uploaded, sent or shared, and only the finished pixel art is kept in the app.

Purchases

Color Puddle is free to download. A grown-up can buy "Unlock everything", a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks all the pictures. It can only be reached in the Grown-ups area, behind a grown-up question. The purchase is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play under your account with them. We never see your name, payment details or account; the app only asks the store whether the unlock has been bought, so it can unlock the pictures (and restore them on another device).

Permissions

When your child taps the Save button, the system share sheet opens. If you choose Save Image, your device asks for permission to add the picture to your photo library. On iPad, taking a photo for pixel art asks for camera permission the first time. Choosing an existing photo uses the system photo picker, which shares only the photo you pick. On Android the app requests no permissions.

Children's privacy

Color Puddle is made for children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). This is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR, Apple's App Store Kids category rules and Google Play's Families policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above. If the app ever started to collect any information, we would update this policy first and ask for a parent's consent where the law requires it.
